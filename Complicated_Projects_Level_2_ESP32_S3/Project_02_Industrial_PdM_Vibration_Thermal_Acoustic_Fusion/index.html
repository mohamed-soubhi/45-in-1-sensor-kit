Project 2: Industrial Predictive Maintenance (PdM) & Machine Health Sentinel

Level 2 Sensor Fusion: ISO 10816 Failure Precursors

In heavy industrial machinery (motors, pumps, turbines), machine failure does not happen instantaneously. It follows a predictable physical degradation curve: Acoustic friction hiss (ultrasonic) → Bearing ball pitting (high Kurtosis) → Severe imbalance (high RMS Velocity) → Thermal breakdown & seizure. This project fuses 4 independent physical domains to prevent catastrophic machine failure.

1. Multi-Core FreeRTOS Task Partitioning

  • Core 1 (500 Hz Real-Time Sampling): High-speed double-buffered vibration sampling of MPU-6050 accelerometer at a precise 2ms period using vTaskDelayUntil.
  • Core 0 (Diagnostics & Fusion Engine): Computes true velocity RMS ($v_{RMS}$ in mm/s), 4th moment statistical Kurtosis, thermal rise rate $\frac{dT}{dt}$, and shaft RPM tachometer over 100ms rolling windows.
  • Hardware Contactor Trip: If vibration exceeds ISO Zone D ($> 7.1\text{ mm/s}$) or bearing temperature shoots upward at $> 5^\circ\text{C/min}$, the 5V Relay trips the main power contactor instantly.

3. Mathematical Fusion Core: Statistical Kurtosis & RMS

Normal smooth machine vibration follows a Gaussian distribution with Kurtosis $\approx 3.0$. When a ball bearing develops an impact pit or spall, sharp transient spikes occur, causing Kurtosis to surge $> 4.5$ weeks before thermal or RMS velocity changes become visible:

$$\text{Kurtosis} = \frac{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \mu)^4}{\sigma^4}$$
