Complicated Projects - Level 1 (Multi-Sensor Fusion)

These advanced embedded systems projects combine 3 to 6 complementary physical sensors and actuators from the Hosyond 45-in-1 Sensor Kit to implement production-grade, non-blocking control loops on the Arduino Uno (ATmega328P).

Why Multi-Sensor Fusion?

Single sensors suffer from noise, optical blinding, thermal delay, and mechanical false triggers. Sensor Fusion combines complementary physical domains with mathematical weighting, rate-of-change derivatives (ΔT/Δt), exponential smoothing (EMA), and hardware interrupts for reliable, fail-safe operation.
